Interview Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before starting your AI-powered interview.

🎯 Welcome to Interview Mirror

You are about to begin a practice interview with our AI-powered Interview Mirror system. Answer each question clearly and confidently. Your responses will be evaluated and included in your interview report.

📷

Camera Access

Please allow camera access when your browser asks for permission. The camera is used during the interview to monitor your interview session and provide a better practice experience.

🎤

Microphone Access

Please allow microphone access so that you can answer the interview questions using your voice. Speak clearly and at a comfortable volume.

🗣️

Speak Clearly

Answer each question in a clear and complete manner. Avoid speaking too quickly and try to explain your answer using simple and professional language.

👁️

Maintain Attention

Try to maintain a natural posture and keep your attention toward the camera. Avoid unnecessary movement during the interview.

❓

Answer One Question at a Time

Listen carefully to each question and provide your answer before moving to the next question. Make sure your answer is related to the question.

🤖

AI Evaluation

Your answers may be evaluated by the AI system based on relevance, correctness and quality. The evaluation will be used to generate your interview report.

🚀 How the Interview Works

1

Start the Interview

Click the Start Interview button below to enter the interview screen.

2

Allow Permissions

Allow camera and microphone permissions when requested by your browser.

3

Listen to the Question

Read or listen carefully to the question displayed by the interview system.

4

Give Your Answer

Speak your answer clearly into the microphone. Take a moment to organize your thoughts before answering.

5

AI Evaluates Your Answer

The system sends your answer for AI evaluation and displays feedback about your response.

6

Continue to the Next Question

After reviewing the evaluation, continue with the next interview question until all questions are completed.

7

View Your Result

After completing the interview, you can view your interview performance and detailed answers in the result/report page.

⚠️ Important Instructions

  • Make sure your camera and microphone are working before starting.
  • Use a stable internet connection for the best experience.
  • Speak clearly and avoid unnecessary background noise.
  • Read each question carefully before answering.
  • Try to provide complete and relevant answers.
  • Avoid refreshing or closing the interview page while an interview is in progress.
  • Complete the interview questions to generate an accurate interview result.

🎤 Ready to Begin?

Make sure you have read all the instructions. When you are ready, start your interview.
